How to watch Malaysia TV for free

You don’t need a paid subscription to watch Malaysia television. These are the legal, free options: live channels in your browser, the broadcasters’ own apps, and free-to-air antenna TV.

Updated 2026-10-12

Quick answer

  1. Watch 8 free Malaysia channels now in Doubo’s web player. No download, no sign-up.
  2. Use the official free apps: RTMKlik, Tonton, sooka.
  3. With an antenna: free-to-air digital TV (DVB-T2).

Official free streaming apps

RTMKlik

Radio Televisyen Malaysia (RTM) · Free · Live TV

Free live streams of RTM channels including TV1, TV2, TV Okey, Berita RTM, TV Sukan, TV6 and RTM Parlimen, plus live radio and catch-up of recent programmes.

Platforms: web, iOS, Android

Tonton

Media Prima · Free tier + paid plans · Live TV

A free ad-supported account gives access to local live TV channels such as TV3 and on-demand shows; the paid TontonUp plan adds premium content.

Platforms: web, iOS, Android

sooka

Astro · Free tier + paid plans · Live TV

After registering a free account, viewers can watch a set of ad-supported channels (such as Astro Awani, Bernama TV and TV Sarawak) and selected shows in standard definition; most sports and premium content need a paid plan.

Platforms: web, iOS, Android, Android TV

Free-to-air antenna TV

Broadcast standard
DVB-T2
Network
MYTV Broadcasting (myFreeview)

Free-to-air digital TV is received with a UHF antenna and a TV with a built-in DVB-T2 tuner or a DVB-T2 decoder, with no subscription fee; MYTV lists about 17 TV channels plus digital radio. Analogue TV was switched off nationwide in 2019, with Sabah and Sarawak the last regions on 31 October 2019.

Questions

Can I watch Malaysia TV online for free?

Yes. Many Malaysia channels stream free and legally. You can watch them in Doubo’s web player, in the broadcasters’ own apps, or over the air with an antenna.

What is the best free app to watch Malaysia TV?

Official options include RTMKlik, Tonton, sooka. Doubo puts the free Malaysia channels from many broadcasters in one place, in the browser or in the app.

Can I watch Malaysia TV from abroad?

Some streams play anywhere, but broadcasters often limit others to viewers inside Malaysia because of their rights. Doubo only lists streams that broadcasters make public and does not bypass these limits.
