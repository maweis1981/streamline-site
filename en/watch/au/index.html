How to watch Australia TV for free

You don’t need a paid subscription to watch Australia television. These are the legal, free options: live channels in your browser, the broadcasters’ own apps, and free-to-air antenna TV.

Updated 2026-10-12

Quick answer

  1. Watch 22 free Australia channels now in Doubo’s web player. No download, no sign-up.
  2. Use the official free apps: ABC iview, SBS On Demand, 7plus.
  3. With an antenna: free-to-air digital TV (DVB-T).

Official free streaming apps

ABC iview

ABC · Free · Live TV

Free live ABC channels and on-demand programmes with no ads; a free ABC Account is required for on-demand programmes.

Platforms: web, iOS, Android, smart TVs, Apple TV, Fire TV, game consoles

SBS On Demand

SBS · Free with ads · Live TV

Free live streams of SBS, SBS VICELAND, SBS Food, SBS World Movies and NITV plus on-demand programmes; a free verified account is required.

Platforms: web, iOS, Android, smart TVs, Apple TV, Chromecast with Google TV

7plus

Seven West Media · Free with ads · Live TV

Free live streams of 7, 7TWO, 7mate, 7Bravo and 7flix plus on-demand sport, shows and movies.

Platforms: iOS, Android, smart TVs

9Now

Nine · Free with ads · Live TV

Free live streams of Channel 9, 9Go!, 9Gem, 9Life and 9Rush plus catch-up; a free account is required.

Platforms: iOS, Android TV

Free-to-air antenna TV

Broadcast standard
DVB-T
Network
Freeview

Free-to-air TV from ABC, SBS, Seven, Nine and Ten is broadcast free over DVB-T and needs an antenna and a digital TV or set-top box; Freeview lists 30+ channels, including digital sub-channels. A Freeview-certified TV with broadband also adds an online guide and catch-up apps.

Good to know

  • Live streams in Australian broadcaster apps are geo-restricted to Australia.
  • ABC live channels can be watched on the iview website without logging in; SBS On Demand live streams follow the NSW schedule.

Questions

Can I watch Australia TV online for free?

Yes. Many Australia channels stream free and legally. You can watch them in Doubo’s web player, in the broadcasters’ own apps, or over the air with an antenna.

What is the best free app to watch Australia TV?

Official options include ABC iview, SBS On Demand, 7plus. Doubo puts the free Australia channels from many broadcasters in one place, in the browser or in the app.

Can I watch Australia TV from abroad?

Some streams play anywhere, but broadcasters often limit others to viewers inside Australia because of their rights. Doubo only lists streams that broadcasters make public and does not bypass these limits.
