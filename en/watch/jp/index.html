How to watch Japan TV for free

You don’t need a paid subscription to watch Japan television. These are the legal, free options: live channels in your browser, the broadcasters’ own apps, and free-to-air antenna TV.

Updated 2026-10-12

Quick answer

  1. Watch 5 free Japan channels now in Doubo’s web player. No download, no sign-up.
  2. Use the official free apps: TVer, ABEMA, NHK WORLD-JAPAN.
  3. With an antenna: free-to-air digital TV (ISDB-T).

Official free streaming apps

TVer

TVer Inc. (official service of the commercial broadcasters) · Free with ads · Live TV

Free catch-up of selected programmes from the commercial broadcasters for a limited period after airing, plus live streams of the five commercial networks focused on prime time; live streams are not available in the TV app.

Platforms: web, iOS, Android, smart TV

ABEMA

AbemaTV, Inc. (CyberAgent and TV Asahi) · Free tier + paid plans · Live TV

About 25 to 30 free linear channels, including a 24-hour news channel, with no registration needed; ABEMA Premium (1,080 yen a month, or 580 yen with ads) adds exclusive content and features.

Platforms: web, iOS, Android

NHK WORLD-JAPAN

NHK · Free · Live TV

NHK's international service streams its English-language TV channel live worldwide for free, with 7-day catch-up for selected programmes.

Platforms: web, iOS, Android

Free-to-air antenna TV

Broadcast standard
ISDB-T
Network
地デジ (Chideji)

Terrestrial digital TV uses ISDB-T on UHF channels 13 to 62; viewers need a TV or tuner for terrestrial digital broadcasting and a UHF antenna. There is no subscription charge, and viewers get NHK General TV, NHK Educational TV and the local commercial stations, with the number of channels varying by region; households with a TV must pay the NHK receiving fee.

Good to know

  • NHK's own simulcast and catch-up service, NHK ONE (successor to NHK+ since 1 October 2025), requires an NHK reception contract; households already paying the fee pay nothing extra.
  • The NHK receiving fee for a terrestrial contract is 1,100 yen a month (965 yen in Okinawa), and an internet-only contract costs the same.
  • TVer and ABEMA are designed for viewers in Japan; TVer's help pages state that its videos cannot be watched from overseas.

Questions

Can I watch Japan TV online for free?

Yes. Many Japan channels stream free and legally. You can watch them in Doubo’s web player, in the broadcasters’ own apps, or over the air with an antenna.

What is the best free app to watch Japan TV?

Official options include TVer, ABEMA, NHK WORLD-JAPAN. Doubo puts the free Japan channels from many broadcasters in one place, in the browser or in the app.

Can I watch Japan TV from abroad?

Some streams play anywhere, but broadcasters often limit others to viewers inside Japan because of their rights. Doubo only lists streams that broadcasters make public and does not bypass these limits.
