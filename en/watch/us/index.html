Free-to-air antenna TV

Broadcast standard
ATSC 1.0 / ATSC 3.0

Local broadcast stations (such as ABC, CBS, NBC, Fox, PBS and their subchannels) are free over the air with an antenna and a TV or converter with an ATSC tuner; the number of channels depends on location. ATSC 3.0 (NEXTGEN TV) needs a compatible tuner, and stations must keep broadcasting in ATSC 1.0, so existing TVs still work.

Good to know

  • The PBS livestream shows only stations for your geographic area, and an account is required on streaming-device apps.
  • Some stations encrypt their ATSC 3.0 signals, which can block reception on some NEXTGEN TV devices; the FCC is reviewing this.

Questions

Can I watch United States TV online for free?

Yes. Many United States channels stream free and legally. You can watch them in Doubo’s web player, in the broadcasters’ own apps, or over the air with an antenna.

What is the best free app to watch United States TV?

Official options include PBS App, Pluto TV, Tubi. Doubo puts the free United States channels from many broadcasters in one place, in the browser or in the app.

Can I watch United States TV from abroad?

Some streams play anywhere, but broadcasters often limit others to viewers inside United States because of their rights. Doubo only lists streams that broadcasters make public and does not bypass these limits.
