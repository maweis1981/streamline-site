How to watch Philippines TV for free

You don’t need a paid subscription to watch Philippines television. These are the legal, free options: live channels in your browser, the broadcasters’ own apps, and free-to-air antenna TV.

Updated 2026-10-12

Quick answer

  1. Watch 4 free Philippines channels now in Doubo’s web player. No download, no sign-up.
  2. Use the official free apps: PTV Livestream, GMA Play, Kapuso Stream.
  3. With an antenna: free-to-air digital TV (ISDB-T).

Official free streaming apps

PTV Livestream

People's Television Network (PTV) · Free · Live TV

The state broadcaster streams its channel live on its website, and also on its official YouTube and Dailymotion channels.

Platforms: web

GMA Play

GMA Network · Free with ads · Live TV

Free app combining live GMA television with on-demand Filipino shows, Asian dramas and movies.

Platforms: web, iOS, Android, Android TV

Kapuso Stream

GMA Network · Free · Live TV

Free simulcast of GMA's afternoon and prime-time programmes on GMA Network's official YouTube and Facebook pages; full episodes are also free on gmanetwork.com.

Platforms: web

iWantTFC

ABS-CBN · Free tier + paid plans

ABS-CBN's streaming service offers some shows free with a sign-up, and in 2026 promoted free viewing of selected Kapamilya programmes; most of the catalogue requires a subscription.

Platforms: web, iOS, Android

Free-to-air antenna TV

Broadcast standard
ISDB-T

Digital terrestrial TV stays free-to-air and is received with an antenna and a TV with an ISDB-T tuner or a digital TV box for older analogue sets. Analogue TV in Mega Manila is scheduled to be switched off on 22 November 2026 under an NTC circular; dates for the rest of the country have not been set.

Questions

Can I watch Philippines TV online for free?

Yes. Many Philippines channels stream free and legally. You can watch them in Doubo’s web player, in the broadcasters’ own apps, or over the air with an antenna.

What is the best free app to watch Philippines TV?

Official options include PTV Livestream, GMA Play, Kapuso Stream. Doubo puts the free Philippines channels from many broadcasters in one place, in the browser or in the app.

Can I watch Philippines TV from abroad?

Some streams play anywhere, but broadcasters often limit others to viewers inside Philippines because of their rights. Doubo only lists streams that broadcasters make public and does not bypass these limits.
