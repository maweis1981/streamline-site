How to watch Hong Kong SAR China TV for free

You don’t need a paid subscription to watch Hong Kong SAR China television. These are the legal, free options: live channels in your browser, the broadcasters’ own apps, and free-to-air antenna TV.

Official free streaming apps

RTHK TV

Radio Television Hong Kong · Free · Live TV

The public broadcaster's free app provides live webcasts of RTHK TV 31 to 35, with video on demand and downloads.

Platforms: web, iOS, Android

myTV SUPER

TVB · Free tier + paid plans · Live TV

TVB's platform marks Jade, TVB Plus and TVB News among its free live channels alongside paid packs; the service is only for use in Hong Kong and Macau.

Platforms: web, iOS, Android

ViuTV

HK Television Entertainment (PCCW) · Free with ads · Live TV

Free live stream of ViuTV (ch.99), with ViuTVsix (ch.96) streamed only at limited times because of rights; Hong Kong only.

Platforms: web, Android

HOY

i-CABLE HOY · Free with ads · Live TV

Free 24-hour live streams of HOY channels 76, 77 and 78 plus on-demand programmes; free streaming works only in Hong Kong.

Platforms: web, iOS, Android, Android TV

Free-to-air antenna TV

Broadcast standard
DTMB

Digital terrestrial TV is based on the national DTMB standard (GB 20600) and has been the only broadcast method since analogue TV was switched off on 30 November 2020. Viewers need a digital TV or set-top box and an antenna, and pay no subscription fee for the free channels from TVB, ViuTV, HOY and RTHK (around 14 channels).

Questions

Can I watch Hong Kong SAR China TV online for free?

Yes. Many Hong Kong SAR China channels stream free and legally. You can watch them in Doubo’s web player, in the broadcasters’ own apps, or over the air with an antenna.

What is the best free app to watch Hong Kong SAR China TV?

Official options include RTHK TV, myTV SUPER, ViuTV. Doubo puts the free Hong Kong SAR China channels from many broadcasters in one place, in the browser or in the app.

Can I watch Hong Kong SAR China TV from abroad?

Some streams play anywhere, but broadcasters often limit others to viewers inside Hong Kong SAR China because of their rights. Doubo only lists streams that broadcasters make public and does not bypass these limits.
