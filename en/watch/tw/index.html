How to watch Taiwan TV for free

You don’t need a paid subscription to watch Taiwan television. These are the legal, free options: live channels in your browser, the broadcasters’ own apps, and free-to-air antenna TV.

Updated 2026-10-12

Quick answer

  1. Watch 4 free Taiwan channels now in Doubo’s web player. No download, no sign-up.
  2. Use the official free apps: 公視+ (PTS+), 公視新聞網 PNN (YouTube), TaiwanPlus.
  3. With an antenna: free-to-air digital TV (DVB-T).

Official free streaming apps

公視+ (PTS+)

Public Television Service (PTS) · Free tier + paid plans · Live TV

The public broadcaster's ad-free platform has free live streams of PTS, PTS Taigi, PTS XS and PTS News, and mostly free on-demand dramas and documentaries; an account is required and a few titles are pay-per-view.

Platforms: web, iOS, Android

公視新聞網 PNN (YouTube)

Public Television Service (PTS) · Free · Live TV

PTS's official YouTube channel streams PTS News 24 hours a day, and PTS also lists official live streams of its main channel and PTS Taigi.

Platforms: YouTube

TaiwanPlus

Public Television Service (PTS), funded by the Ministry of Culture · Free · Live TV

Free English-language news, documentaries and a live channel from Taiwan, ad-free and available worldwide.

Platforms: web, iOS, Android, Apple TV, Android TV

華視新聞 (CTS News)

Chinese Television System (CTS) · Free · Live TV

CTS's free news app includes a 24-hour live news stream.

Platforms: iOS

Free-to-air antenna TV

Broadcast standard
DVB-T

Free digital terrestrial TV uses DVB-T and has fully replaced analogue since 30 June 2012; viewers need a digital TV antenna and a TV or set-top box with HD decoding, since several channels are in HD. It is free and carries about 20 channels from the five terrestrial broadcasters (TTV, CTV, CTS, FTV and PTS).

Good to know

  • Terrestrial signals are also relayed by satellite as a free alternative for areas with poor terrestrial reception.
  • Some commercial news channels have stopped their 24-hour YouTube live streams in Taiwan, so availability on YouTube can change.

Questions

Can I watch Taiwan TV online for free?

Yes. Many Taiwan channels stream free and legally. You can watch them in Doubo’s web player, in the broadcasters’ own apps, or over the air with an antenna.

What is the best free app to watch Taiwan TV?

Official options include 公視+ (PTS+), 公視新聞網 PNN (YouTube), TaiwanPlus. Doubo puts the free Taiwan channels from many broadcasters in one place, in the browser or in the app.

Can I watch Taiwan TV from abroad?

Some streams play anywhere, but broadcasters often limit others to viewers inside Taiwan because of their rights. Doubo only lists streams that broadcasters make public and does not bypass these limits.
