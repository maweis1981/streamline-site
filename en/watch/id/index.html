You don’t need a paid subscription to watch Indonesia television. These are the legal, free options: live channels in your browser, the broadcasters’ own apps, and free-to-air antenna TV.

Official free streaming apps

TVRI Klik

Televisi Republik Indonesia (TVRI) · Free · Live TV

Free live streams of the public broadcaster's national channels (including TVRI Nasional and TVRI Sport) and regional stations, plus on-demand replays.

Platforms: web, iOS, Android

RCTI+

MNC Group · Free with ads · Live TV

Free ad-supported live streams of RCTI, MNCTV, GTV and iNews, with catch-up of recent programmes.

Platforms: web, iOS, Android, Android TV

Vidio

Emtek (Elang Mahkota Teknologi) · Free tier + paid plans · Live TV

Free-to-air channels such as SCTV and Indosiar can be streamed live for free with ads; premium channels, many sports matches and ad-free viewing need a paid plan.

Platforms: web, Android, Android TV

Free-to-air antenna TV

Broadcast standard
DVB-T2

Free-to-air digital TV needs an antenna and a TV with a DVB-T2 tuner, or a DVB-T2 set-top box for an older analogue TV; watching is free. Analogue broadcasts were switched off region by region between 2022 and 2023, and the government distributed free set-top boxes to low-income households.

Questions

Can I watch Indonesia TV online for free?

Yes. Many Indonesia channels stream free and legally. You can watch them in Doubo’s web player, in the broadcasters’ own apps, or over the air with an antenna.

What is the best free app to watch Indonesia TV?

Official options include TVRI Klik, RCTI+, Vidio. Doubo puts the free Indonesia channels from many broadcasters in one place, in the browser or in the app.

Can I watch Indonesia TV from abroad?

Some streams play anywhere, but broadcasters often limit others to viewers inside Indonesia because of their rights. Doubo only lists streams that broadcasters make public and does not bypass these limits.
