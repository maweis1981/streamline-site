How to watch South Korea TV for free

You don’t need a paid subscription to watch South Korea television. These are the legal, free options: live channels in your browser, the broadcasters’ own apps, and free-to-air antenna TV.

Official free streaming apps

KBS+

KBS · Free tier + paid plans · Live TV

Public broadcaster app with KBS live channels and on-demand replays; the app is free, and some replays are paid.

Platforms: iOS, Android

MBC

MBC · Free tier + paid plans · Live TV

Free live streams of seven MBC channels including MBC, MBC every1 and MBC DRAMA; many replays need a paid pass.

Platforms: web, iOS

SBS play

SBS · Free tier + paid plans · Live TV

Free live SBS, SBS Plus, SBS Sports and SBS Golf for logged-in members (non-members get a 3-minute preview), plus some free replays.

Platforms: iOS

EBS play

EBS · Free tier + paid plans · Live TV

Free live streams of six EBS channels including EBS1; full replay access is paid.

Platforms: iOS

Free-to-air antenna TV

Broadcast standard
ATSC 1.0 / ATSC 3.0

The terrestrial channels of KBS, MBC, SBS and EBS are free over the air with an antenna: HD in ATSC 1.0 and 4K UHD in ATSC 3.0, which needs a UHD TV with an ATSC 3.0 tuner. Many apartment buildings distribute signals through a shared rooftop antenna system, and older systems may not pass ATSC 3.0 signals.

Questions

Can I watch South Korea TV online for free?

Yes. Many South Korea channels stream free and legally. You can watch them in Doubo’s web player, in the broadcasters’ own apps, or over the air with an antenna.

What is the best free app to watch South Korea TV?

Official options include KBS+, MBC, SBS play. Doubo puts the free South Korea channels from many broadcasters in one place, in the browser or in the app.

Can I watch South Korea TV from abroad?

Some streams play anywhere, but broadcasters often limit others to viewers inside South Korea because of their rights. Doubo only lists streams that broadcasters make public and does not bypass these limits.
