How to watch India TV for free

You don’t need a paid subscription to watch India television. These are the legal, free options: live channels in your browser, the broadcasters’ own apps, and free-to-air antenna TV.

Official free streaming apps

WAVES

Prasar Bharati · Free · Live TV

Public broadcaster app with about 140 live TV channels, including all Doordarshan satellite channels, plus radio and on-demand titles; almost all content is free, with a few premium items.

Platforms: web, iOS, Android, smart TVs, Fire TV, Chromecast

Amazon MX Player

Amazon · Free with ads

Free ad-supported service with Indian original series, films and shows, also reachable through the Amazon shopping app and Prime Video.

Platforms: iOS, Android, smart TVs, Fire TV

Samsung TV Plus

Samsung · Free with ads · Live TV

Free ad-supported live channels (Samsung cites 150+) in news, movies, music and entertainment, available only on Samsung devices.

Platforms: Samsung smart TVs, select Galaxy phones and tablets

Free-to-air antenna TV

Broadcast standard
DVB-T2

Doordarshan runs a free-to-air DVB-T2 digital terrestrial service, but only in a limited number of cities (Prasar Bharati lists 19), and it needs a DVB-T2 set-top box or a TV with a DVB-T2 tuner plus an antenna. Most free TV viewing in India is via DD Free Dish, Prasar Bharati's free satellite service, which has no monthly or annual fee after a one-time purchase of a dish and set-top box (about Rs 1,500 to 2,000) and carries 160+ TV channels.

Good to know

  • DD Free Dish is free for life with no subscription; Prasar Bharati recommends authorised set-top boxes because it may restrict MPEG-4 channels on unauthorised ones.
  • DD Free Dish channel counts change after each annual slot auction; check Prasar Bharati's current list.

Questions

Can I watch India TV online for free?

Yes. Many India channels stream free and legally. You can watch them in Doubo’s web player, in the broadcasters’ own apps, or over the air with an antenna.

What is the best free app to watch India TV?

Official options include WAVES, Amazon MX Player, Samsung TV Plus. Doubo puts the free India channels from many broadcasters in one place, in the browser or in the app.

Can I watch India TV from abroad?

Some streams play anywhere, but broadcasters often limit others to viewers inside India because of their rights. Doubo only lists streams that broadcasters make public and does not bypass these limits.
