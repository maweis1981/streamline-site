Official free streaming apps

Thai PBS

Thai Public Broadcasting Service (Thai PBS) · Free · Live TV

The public broadcaster streams its channel live for free on its website and app, with AI-generated live captions and on-demand programmes.

Platforms: web, iOS, Android

VIPA

Thai Public Broadcasting Service (Thai PBS) · Free

Thai PBS's on-demand platform offers dramas, documentaries and Thai PBS programmes for free with no ads.

Platforms: web, iOS, Android

CH3 Plus

BEC World (Channel 3) · Free tier + paid plans · Live TV

Channel 3 can be watched live and on catch-up for free with ads; the paid 3Plus Premium tier removes ads and adds extras.

Platforms: web, iOS, Android

TrueID

True Digital Group · Free tier + paid plans · Live TV

Free-to-air channels such as Thai PBS, Channel 3 and Channel 7 are listed among TrueID's free channels; premium TrueVisions channels need a paid package.

Platforms: web, iOS, Android, Android TV

Free-to-air antenna TV

Broadcast standard
DVB-T2

Free-to-air digital TV is received with an antenna and a TV with a built-in DVB-T2 tuner or a DVB-T2 set-top box, with no subscription fee. Analogue broadcasting ended in 2020, when Channel 3 became the last analogue channel to go off air on 25 March 2020; commercial digital TV licences run until 2029.

Questions

Can I watch Thailand TV online for free?

Yes. Many Thailand channels stream free and legally. You can watch them in Doubo’s web player, in the broadcasters’ own apps, or over the air with an antenna.

What is the best free app to watch Thailand TV?

Official options include Thai PBS, VIPA, CH3 Plus. Doubo puts the free Thailand channels from many broadcasters in one place, in the browser or in the app.

Can I watch Thailand TV from abroad?

Some streams play anywhere, but broadcasters often limit others to viewers inside Thailand because of their rights. Doubo only lists streams that broadcasters make public and does not bypass these limits.
