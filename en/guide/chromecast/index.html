How to Cast M3U Playlists to Chromecast and Google TV

Chromecast cannot open playlists by itself, but a computer or phone can send channels to it in a few taps.

What you need

  • A Chromecast or Google TV on the same Wi-Fi network
  • Google Chrome on a computer, or an Android phone
  • An M3U / M3U8 playlist link

Steps

  1. 01

    Open the web player in Chrome

    On your computer, open doubo.tv/web in Chrome. Pick a free channel or add your own playlist link.

  2. 02

    Cast the tab

    Open Chrome’s menu ⋮ → Cast (in some versions under Save and share), choose your Chromecast, and play the channel full screen.

  3. 03

    From an Android phone: VLC

    Open the playlist in VLC for Android, start a channel, tap the cast icon and choose your Chromecast.

Tips

  • Keep the casting tab in the foreground on your computer for the smoothest playback.
  • Google TV models can also install VLC directly; see the Android TV guide.

Try it in your browser first

No install needed. Doubo’s web player opens free live channels right away, and plays your own M3U links too.

Questions

Does casting a Chrome tab lower quality?

Tab casting re-encodes the picture, so quality depends on your computer and Wi-Fi. For the best quality, install a player on Google TV.

Can I cast from an iPhone to Chromecast?

Chromecast does not support AirPlay. Use a computer with Chrome or an Android phone instead.
