What we list

  • Only streams served from the channel’s own website, a broadcast-grade CDN or an official free streaming platform.
  • No video is stored, transcoded or relayed, and no paid channels are listed.
  • Adult content and channels that rights holders have objected to are left out.
  • The generator and the rules are public on GitHub.

Channel data comes from the open-source iptv-org project, filtered by stricter rules. Rights holders can ask for a channel to be removed; requests are handled within 72 hours.
