Roadmap

Roadmap

We focus on a smooth, stable playback experience first, then expand features and platforms. This is our planned direction, not a promise of release dates.

Last updated: October 2026

Shipped

1.0 Foundation

  • M3U/M3U8 import and history restore

    Import from a URL or file; recent playlists are remembered.

  • Groups, search, favorites, recently played

  • Parallel availability checks

  • Picture in Picture, subtitles, playback speed

  • Switch between same-name channel lines

  • DLNA casting and local video

In progress

Smoother playback

  • Broader HTTP stream compatibility

  • Background audio and Picture in Picture polish

  • Richer M3U parsing

    GBK and other encodings, tvg-id, per-channel User-Agent / Referer headers.

  • Channel health status

    Shows each channel’s live status, with cached results.

Next

Reliability and speed

  • Android app

    Android phones, tablets, and Android TV / TV boxes.

  • Multi-line channels with smart switching

    Automatically picks the smoothest line.

  • Cached remote playlists and offline open

  • AirPlay

  • Performance for 10,000+ channel lists

  • App localization

    English, Simplified Chinese, Bahasa Melayu, Bahasa Indonesia, Tiếng Việt, and Thai.

Later

More content and platforms

  • XMLTV program guide (EPG)

  • Catch-up

    Available only when the stream source provides catch-up parameters.

  • iCloud sync

    Favorites, playlists, and settings across devices.

  • Xtream Codes support

  • Apple TV and CarPlay

  • Global search
