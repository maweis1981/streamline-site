M3U tools

M3U playlist analyzer & cleaner

Paste a link, import a file or paste the playlist. See what’s inside in seconds, then export a tidy copy.

What this tool does

  • Counts entries, channels and groups, and how many entries have a logo or a tvg-id.
  • Finds exact duplicate stream links and removes them in one click.
  • Sorts by group and channel name and exports a clean M3U file.
  • Keeps only HTTPS links, which play in more browsers and devices.

Frequently asked questions

Does this tool check whether links play?

No. It only reads the playlist text to count and tidy it, and never opens any stream. To watch, use the Doubo web player or app.

How large can the playlist be?

Playlists with tens of thousands of entries are processed in seconds, depending on your device.

Is my playlist saved?

No. Everything is processed in your browser’s memory and cleared when you close the page.
